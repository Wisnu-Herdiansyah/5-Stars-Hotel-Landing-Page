OTA mengambil 15–25% komisi dari setiap pemesanan. Dengan booking langsung, kami bisa menawarkan harga lebih rendah dan manfaat eksklusif tanpa platform ketiga.
Paket eksklusif hanya di sini
Trans Studio Family Escape Package — tiket taman bermain, sarapan, dan late checkout — tidak tersedia di Booking.com, Agoda, atau Traveloka. Hanya di sini.
Respon langsung via WhatsApp
Hubungi tim kami langsung via WhatsApp untuk permintaan khusus, upgrade kamar, atau konfirmasi — bukan chatbot OTA yang lambat merespons.
Upgrade prioritas
Tamu yang memesan langsung mendapatkan prioritas upgrade kamar apabila tersedia, serta early check-in tanpa biaya tambahan.
Kebijakan perubahan lebih fleksibel
Perubahan atau pembatalan reservasi ditangani langsung oleh tim kami — lebih cepat, lebih fleksibel, dan tanpa kebijakan kaku dari pihak OTA.

OTA mengenakan biaya layanan tersembunyi yang ditambahkan saat checkout. Harga yang Anda lihat di sini adalah harga yang Anda bayar.

Why this page outperforms OTA listings — 9 specific reasons

1
Urgency & scarcity trigger

The "3 kamar tersisa" badge creates real purchase urgency. OTA listings bury this in small text. Here it's a pulsing red badge in the hero — the highest-converting psychological trigger in hotel booking.

2
Exclusive package not on OTAs

The Trans Studio bundle (room + tickets + breakfast + late CO) is only available direct. OTA can't compete with something they don't have. This is the most powerful direct booking incentive possible.

3
WhatsApp CTA — mobile conversion

70%+ of Indonesian hotel searches happen on mobile. A floating WhatsApp button is the highest-converting mobile CTA in the Indonesian market — OTAs offer none. This alone can recover 10–15% lost bookings.

4
Transparent OTA price comparison

Showing the OTA price with strikethrough next to the direct price removes the "maybe OTA is cheaper" doubt. It frames the decision before the guest even thinks to comparison shop.

5
Segment-specific headline & copy

OTA listings use generic room descriptions. This page speaks directly to Jakarta families — "untuk keluarga," "anak-anak," "4 orang." Specific copy converts. Generic copy loses.

6
USP proof — not just claims

The "Why Trans Luxury" section turns physical assets (beach pool, 18F view, Trans Studio access) into specific guest benefits. OTA listings list room features. This page sells experiences.

7
Package selector reduces decision paralysis

Instead of a dropdown of 20 room types (OTA standard), this page offers 3 clear packages with prices visible upfront. Fewer choices = higher conversion. Pricing transparency builds trust.

8
Social proof with specificity

Reviews reference specific hotel features ("Trans Studio ada di sebelah") rather than generic praise. Specific reviews are 3x more convincing than "great hotel!" OTA reviews are unmoderated noise.

9
Zero commission = competitive price room

Cutting the 20% OTA commission allows the hotel to price the direct package lower OR include more value at the same price point. This math always works in direct booking's favor.
